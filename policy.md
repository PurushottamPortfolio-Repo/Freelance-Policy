## 1. Project Consultation

Every project begins with a consultation to understand your requirements, objectives, preferred timeline, and budget.

Before development begins, you will receive a project proposal containing:

* Project scope
* Deliverables
* Estimated timeline
* Project investment
* Payment milestones
* Project-specific terms

There is no obligation to proceed until the proposal is reviewed and approved.

---

---

## 3. Project Scope

The approved proposal defines the project's scope, deliverables, features, and services.

The project investment covers the work included in the approved scope.

Additional requirements can be requested at any stage. Before implementation, any change that affects the project's cost or timeline will be communicated and approved.

---

## 4. Payment

The standard payment structure is:

### Standard Projects

* **30% — Project Booking**
* **40% — Development Milestone**
* **30% — Final Delivery & Deployment**

### Small Projects

A **50% + 50%** payment structure may be used.

### Large Projects

Larger projects may use multiple milestone payments based on the agreed deliverables.

The applicable payment schedule will always be specified in the project proposal.

Development work begins after the applicable initial payment has been received.

---

## 5. Design & Revisions

The number of included revision rounds will be specified in the project proposal.

Included revisions may cover reasonable refinements to the approved design, such as:

* Text changes
* Image replacement
* Spacing adjustments
* Colour adjustments
* Minor layout refinements

New pages, features, workflows, redesigns, or major structural changes may be treated as additional work.

Any additional cost or timeline impact will be communicated before implementation.

---

## 6. Project Timeline

The estimated delivery timeline will be provided in the project proposal.

The timeline depends on:

* Requirement clarity
* Content and asset availability
* Client feedback
* Design approvals
* Third-party services
* External integrations
* Approved scope changes

Delays caused by pending client information, feedback, approvals, or third-party dependencies may affect the delivery timeline.

---

## 7. Quality Assurance

Projects are reviewed before delivery according to their scope.

Quality checks may include:

* Responsive design
* Mobile, tablet, and desktop compatibility
* Major browser compatibility
* Navigation and links
* Form functionality
* Performance
* Basic SEO implementation
* Basic security checks
* UI consistency
* Production deployment verification

---

## 8. Delivery & Handover

After the agreed project amount has been paid in full, the final deliverables will be provided according to the project agreement.

Depending on the project, handover may include:

* Complete Walkthrough
* Basic Documentation
* Configuration information
* Required access details
* Basic usage guidance

Third-party software, plugins, libraries, fonts, assets, APIs, and services remain subject to their respective licences and terms.

---

## 9. Support & Warranty

Post-delivery support will be provided according to the project proposal.

Warranty support covers issues related to the delivered implementation, including applicable:

* Functional bugs
* Implementation errors
* Broken agreed functionality
* Reasonable compatibility issues

Warranty support does not include:

* New features
* New pages
* Major redesigns
* Client-side modifications
* New integrations
* Changes to third-party services
* New requirements outside the approved scope

Additional requirements can be requested as future enhancements.

---

## 10. Third-Party Services

Third-party services are the client's responsibility unless specifically included in the project quotation.

These may include:

* Premium plugins or themes
* Paid APIs
* Email or SMS services
* Payment gateway charges
* SaaS subscriptions
* Software licences
* Stock images or other paid assets

Assistance with configuration and integration may be provided where included in the project scope.

---

## 11. Cancellation

Either party may request cancellation of a project.

If a project is cancelled after work has started:

* Completed work remains payable.
* Work already performed will be evaluated according to the agreed scope and milestones.
* Any applicable balance or refund will be determined based on completed and pending work.
* Project files or deliverables will be transferred according to the payment status and project agreement.

Project-specific cancellation terms may be included in the proposal or project agreement.

---

## 12. Confidentiality

Confidential business information, project information, credentials, documents, and materials shared privately during the project will be treated as confidential.

Confidentiality does not apply to information that:

* Is already publicly available
* Becomes publicly available without breach of this policy
* Was independently developed
* Must be disclosed by law

---

## 13. Communication

To keep projects organised, important project decisions should be confirmed through written communication such as:

- Email
- Project management tools
- Approved messaging channels

This helps both sides maintain a clear record of requirements, approvals and decisions.

Written confirmation may be used to maintain a clear record of:

* Requirements
* Approvals
* Scope changes
* Project decisions
* Delivery milestones

---

## 14. Scope Changes

Project requirements may evolve during development.

The process for additional requirements is:

**Request → Review → Estimate → Approval → Implementation**

No additional work affecting the agreed project cost will be started without prior communication and approval.

---

## 15. Client Responsibilities

The client is responsible for providing, where applicable:

* Required business information
* Website or application content
* Images and other assets
* Required access credentials
* Timely feedback
* Approvals
* Third-party account access

Delays in providing required information or approvals may affect the project schedule.

## 17. Security & Credentials

Client credentials and access information provided for project development will be handled responsibly and used only for the agreed project requirements.

Clients are responsible for maintaining ownership and billing of their third-party accounts, domains, hosting, APIs, and services unless otherwise agreed.

---

## 18. Portfolio & Project Showcase

Unless confidentiality or non-disclosure terms state otherwise, completed projects may be displayed in the freelancer's portfolio for professional and promotional purposes.

If a project is confidential, portfolio usage will not be made public without the client's permission.

---

## 19. Acceptance & Approval

Project milestones, designs, and deliverables may require client approval.

Once a milestone or deliverable has been approved, subsequent work will proceed based on that approval.

Significant changes requested(CR) after approval may be treated as additional work.

---

## 20. Professional Commitment

Every project is handled with a focus on:

* Clear communication
* Transparent pricing
* Practical solutions
* Quality development
* Reasonable timelines
* Client collaboration
* Long-term maintainability

The objective is to build a solution that is useful for the client's business, not simply to complete a checklist of features.
